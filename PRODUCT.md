# Product

## Register

product

## Users

ScoutIQ is for NBA front-office decision makers: GMs, cap strategists, analysts, and scouts evaluating player value, contract structure, and roster flexibility. They use it in high-context decision sessions where credibility, speed, uncertainty, and source traceability matter more than decorative flourish.

## Product Purpose

ScoutIQ helps a front office decide what a player is worth, what a proposed contract does to cap and apron flexibility, and why the recommendation is credible. Success means the interface makes calibrated model output, scouting context, and cap mechanics feel like one explainable decision cockpit rather than separate dashboards.

## Brand Personality

Precise, cinematic, and intellectually aggressive. ScoutIQ should feel like a front-office war room built around evidence: sharp, distinctive, and analytical without becoming noisy or gimmicky.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS dashboard patterns: stacked white cards, flat gray tables, timid accent colors, identical metric tiles, and motionless admin chrome. Avoid sports-betting aesthetics, fan-site clutter, overused navy-and-gold sports palettes, fake glassmorphism, and decorative data visuals that do not improve decision-making.

## Design Principles

1. Make the decision state visible before the data inventory.
2. Treat uncertainty as a premium feature, not a caveat.
3. Give contract math a physical shape: timelines, bands, pressure zones, and consequences.
4. Fuse scouting, value, and cap context into one read instead of siloed panels.
5. Use motion and color to clarify changes, ranking, risk, and confidence, not to decorate static chrome.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast for text and controls. Preserve keyboard access for search, navigation, tabs, and contract controls. Support reduced motion with simplified transitions. Do not rely on color alone for positive, negative, warning, or confidence states.
